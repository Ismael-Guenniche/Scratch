SCRATCH

Privacy policy · Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Español

WHAT SCRATCH IS

Scratch is a local music player for Android. It plays the audio files you have on your phone. It has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.

WHAT DATA IT KEEPS

Everything is stored only on your device: the music folder you choose, the list of scanned songs, your playlists, favorites, listening history and statistics, song tags and your settings. None of this is sent to anyone, except what the optional features you turn on (below) need.

PERMISSIONS IT USES

Music: Scratch does not ask for access to all your storage. You pick a folder with the system file picker and the app only reads that folder (and writes to it only if you save tags into a file with the editor).

Notifications: to show the playback controls in the status bar and on the lock screen.

Foreground service and keep-awake: so the music keeps playing with the screen off.

Internet: only for optional features you turn on: Spotify, searching lyrics on lrclib.net and sending your listens to ListenBrainz (title, artist, album and time, with your token), and to open search links in your browser.

SPOTIFY (OPTIONAL)

If you use "Import from Spotify", the app connects to Spotify with your account to read only your playlists (song and artist names) and match them with your local files. Credentials and tokens are stored only on your phone and you can delete them by signing out in the app or uninstalling it. Scratch does not download audio from Spotify or any other service. Spotify is a third party with its own privacy policy.

LISTENBRAINZ (OPTIONAL)

If you turn on scrobbling in Settings and paste your ListenBrainz token, each song you listen to (title, artist, album, duration and time) is sent to your ListenBrainz account. You can turn it off at any time; anything waiting to be sent is stored only on your phone.

SHARING DATA

Scratch does not sell your data or share it with anyone. Only the data each service needs leaves your phone, and only to the services you turn on (Spotify, lrclib.net for lyrics and ListenBrainz).

DELETING YOUR DATA

You can delete everything from system Settings → Apps → Scratch → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Your music files are never touched.

CHILDREN

Scratch is not aimed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes in a significant way, the app will ask you to accept it again.
